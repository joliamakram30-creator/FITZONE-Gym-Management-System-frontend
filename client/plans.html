Common Inquiries

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Can I freeze my membership if I travel?

Yes! 3-month and 6-month plans can be frozen for up to 14 days, and Annual memberships can be frozen for up to 30 days without any penalty.

What payment methods do you accept in Egypt?

We accept Visa, Mastercard, Cash at reception, InstaPay transfers, and Vodafone Cash mobile wallet payments.

How do I access the gym using my digital card?

Once you join, simply open your FITZONE Member Portal on your smartphone. The reception scanner reads your dynamic QR code instantly upon entry.
